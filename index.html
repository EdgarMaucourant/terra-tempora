      Sources

      • Borders: Cliopatria, Seshat Global History Databank, CC BY 4.0: the realms of 3400 BCE to 2024, simplified for display. Today’s countries are its realms of 2024, drawn until the current year.
      • Borders of Magadha and its successors (the Nanda, Maurya and Shunga empires, 600 to 171 BCE): traced from Magadha Expansion by Avantiputra7, after Schwartzberg’s Historical Atlas of South Asia, CC BY-SA 4.0; the traced borders are shared under the same licence.
      • Borders of the Bamana Empire of Ségou (1766 to 1802), Kaarta, Kong and the Mossi and Hausa kingdoms: traced from Africa de l’Oèst (fin sègle XVIII) by Nicolas Eynaud, CC BY-SA 4.0; the traced borders are shared under the same licence.
      • Borders of the Luba Empire: traced from Lunda Empire, Library of Congress (Zaire: A Country Study, 1994, after Jan Vansina’s Kingdoms of the Savanna), public domain.
      • Borders of the Kingdom of Great Zimbabwe: taken from African civilizations map (pre-colonial) by Jeff Israel, CC BY-SA 3.0; the borders are shared under the same licence.
      • Civilization names in other languages, key figures and links between realms: Wikidata, CC0.
      • Card pictures: the realm’s picture, coat of arms or flag on Wikidata, or else the lead picture of its English Wikipedia article; every file is on Wikimedia Commons and is credited with its author and licence.
      • Realm overviews: the opening paragraph of the realm’s Wikipedia article, in the reader’s language when there is one (CC BY-SA 4.0), with the photos and videos the article shows, kept only when they are on Wikimedia Commons, each credited with its author and licence.
      • Events: the Mongol ones written for this site from standard references; the others from Wikidata (CC0), with the opening paragraph of their Wikipedia article (CC BY-SA 4.0); pictures from the events’ Wikipedia articles, shown only when the file is on Wikimedia Commons, with its author and licence.
      • History travel: the turning points were chosen and written for this site from standard references; each links to its Wikipedia article and to searches on Britannica and World History Encyclopedia.
      • Documentaries: hand-picked, each linked to its page on Netflix, National Geographic, Discovery Channel or History Channel.
      • Globe imagery: Natural Earth II, public domain, as packaged with CesiumJS (Apache 2.0).
      • Map coastlines: Natural Earth 1:50m land, public domain, via world-atlas (ISC), read with topojson-client (ISC). Projections by d3-geo and d3-geo-projection (ISC).
      • Fonts: Newsreader (Production Type) and Instrument Sans (Instrument), SIL Open Font License 1.1, served with the site through Fontsource.
      • Hosting: Cloudflare Workers, which also keeps the anonymous usage counts and checks reports for spam (Turnstile). The privacy and legal notice has the details.
